Why Orbit exists

The questions families ask every day

🕑

"Did anyone give Papa the 2 pm tablet?"

You're in a meeting. In Orbit every dose has a named person, and you see who marked it and when. If nobody does, the giver is reminded, and then the whole family is told.

🩺

"How often did the tremor happen this month?"

The doctor asks and nobody remembers. One-tap care notes sit next to the dose record, so the brief before a visit shows missed evening doses and the bad days side by side.

💊

"We ran out of Amma's Levodopa on Sunday."

Orbit counts down the tablets with each dose given. When about 5 days are left, the family gets an email with one-tap links to the chemist and online pharmacies.

One profile, three views

Built around one person, shared by everyone who cares

For your parent: the bedside screen

  • No reading, no typing. At dose time it speaks, and the whole screen is one button.
  • Help needs a 2-second hold, so a brushing hand doesn't raise an alarm.
  • One picture question a day: how are you feeling?
  • Turns warm amber at night, so it doesn't disturb their sleep.
  • Stays awake on its stand, so it never sleeps through a dose.

For family and nurse

  • Each person has their own passcode and role.
  • "About them": what they like and what calms them, the first thing a new nurse sees.
  • Doses are grouped by morning, afternoon, evening and night, each with the person who gives it.
  • Refill bars, checkup reminders and an evening update.
  • Call the ambulance, hospital or doctor in one tap.

For the doctor

  • Read-only access.
  • A printable brief: adherence by medicine and time of day.
  • Missed doses and symptoms on one timeline, day by day.
  • Call and home-visit requests arrive with their current medicines.
