For him

A tablet by the bed. No reading, no typing. At dose time it speaks and the whole screen becomes one button. A Help button tells everyone at once. One picture question a day: how are you feeling?

For family and nurse

Every dose has a named person who gives it. Missed doses go to that person first, then to the whole family. Medicines, refills, checkups, side-effect notes and an evening update.

For the doctor

Read-only. Adherence by medicine and time of day, with tremor, confusion and falls next to the doses, written up from the record before each visit.

How reminders work

  1. 15 minutes before a dose, the person giving it gets an email.
  2. 30 minutes after, if it isn't marked, they are reminded.
  3. After 60 minutes, the whole family is told.
  4. Appointments: a week before and the day before. Regular checkups: when one is due and nothing is booked.
  5. Refills: when about 5 days of tablets are left.

Every rule is fixed code you can read in the repository. The AI only writes the words of summaries, and its text is checked against the record.

Orbit is a prototype built for the Catalyst hackathon. It does not replace medical advice or emergency services. In an emergency, call 112.
